How it works

From analyser to authority,
under five seconds.

One resilient data path keeps every sample buffered, validated, visible and ready for regulatory submission.

  1. 01Measure

    Reference analysers and the meteorological mast sample continuously.

  2. 02Acquire

    Multi-protocol DAS buffers, timestamps and tags every channel.

  3. 03Validate

    Calibration states and QA flags separate reportable data.

  4. 04Watch

    Operators see AQI, trends, drift and availability in one view.

  5. 05Report

    Signed telemetry and acknowledgements reach CPCB and SPCB.
