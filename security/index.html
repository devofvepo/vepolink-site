Security & Trust

Security Statement

Protecting Information. Building Trust. Our security approach focuses on responsible information handling, risk reduction, and maintaining system integrity across our industrial monitoring solutions and digital services.

Effective Date: 2025 Last Updated: 2026

On This Page

1. Our Security Approach

At Vepolink, security and reliability are important considerations in delivering industrial monitoring solutions and digital services. We recognize the importance of protecting business information, supporting reliable system operations, and maintaining the integrity of information handled through our website and services.

We aim to apply appropriate security practices across our digital platforms and business operations, taking into account the nature of the information processed and the risks associated with the relevant systems. Our approach may include access management, secure system configuration, software maintenance, monitoring, and incident response procedures, as applicable to the service concerned.

2. Website Security

We aim to protect our website and associated services against unauthorized access, misuse, and disruption. Depending on the platform and service, security measures may include:

  • Secure communication using HTTPS.
  • Access restrictions for administrative functions.
  • Software and dependency updates.
  • Appropriate hosting and configuration controls.
  • Monitoring for suspicious activity.
  • Backup and recovery arrangements, where implemented.

The specific measures in place may vary across systems and may change as our infrastructure evolves.

3. Data Protection and Confidentiality

We recognize that customer inquiries, project details, and business communications may contain confidential information. We seek to handle such information responsibly and restrict access to authorized personnel and service providers where appropriate.

Customers should avoid submitting passwords, authentication tokens, financial credentials, or other highly sensitive information through general website inquiry forms.

4. Industrial Monitoring and Connected Systems

Industrial monitoring solutions may involve field instruments, data acquisition systems, communication networks, cloud platforms, dashboards, and reporting interfaces. The security of these environments depends on the configuration and operation of individual components and the overall system architecture.

Where applicable to the supplied solution, security considerations may include:

  • Restricting access to authorized users.
  • Protecting communication between system components.
  • Applying appropriate device and account configurations.
  • Maintaining supported software and firmware.
  • Reviewing network connectivity and external integrations.
  • Monitoring system health and investigating unusual activity.

The measures available for a particular deployment depend on the supplied equipment, third-party components, network environment, and agreed project scope.

5. Customer Responsibilities

Customers and authorized system operators also play an important role in maintaining security. We recommend that customers:

  • Use strong, unique passwords.
  • Limit access according to individual responsibilities.
  • Protect administrative accounts and credentials.
  • Keep supported devices and software updated.
  • Secure their local networks and remote access connections.
  • Review user permissions periodically.
  • Report suspected security incidents promptly.

Customers are responsible for securing systems, networks, and equipment under their own administrative control, subject to the responsibilities agreed in the applicable contract.

6. Vulnerability and Security Issue Reporting

We encourage responsible reporting of potential security vulnerabilities or suspected security incidents affecting our website or services. When reporting an issue, please provide a description of the affected service, the nature of the concern, and sufficient details to help us investigate.

Please do not include passwords, private keys, personal identification documents, or unnecessary confidential customer information in your report.

Security Contact: support@vepolink.com

We will review reports and determine appropriate follow-up actions based on the nature, severity, and scope of the issue.

7. Security Incident Management

If a security incident affecting our systems or services is identified, we aim to assess the issue and take reasonable steps to investigate, contain, and address it.

Where required, we will provide notifications in accordance with applicable legal obligations and contractual commitments. Response timelines and responsibilities may vary depending on the severity of the incident and the service agreement in place.

8. Third-Party Services

Our website and solutions may rely on third-party hosting providers, communication services, software platforms, or equipment.

The security practices of these providers may be governed by their own policies and contractual arrangements. Where appropriate, we consider relevant third-party dependencies when assessing the security of a service.

9. Continuous Improvement

Security requirements and technology continue to evolve.

We aim to review relevant practices periodically and improve our approach as appropriate to our business, technical capabilities, and applicable requirements.
