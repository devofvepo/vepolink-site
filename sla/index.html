Service Level Agreement (SLA)

General service principles, support hours, incident prioritization, response targets, and maintenance terms applicable to Vepolink products and services.

Effective Date: 2025 Last Updated: 2026

On This Page

1. Introduction 2. Scope of Services 3. Support Availability 4. Response Targets 5. Service Availability 6. Incident Reporting 7. Incident Escalation 8. Planned Maintenance 9. Customer Responsibilities 10. Exclusions 11. Service Reporting 12. Changes to SLA 13. Contact Information

1. Introduction

This Service Level Agreement (SLA) describes the general service and support principles applicable to eligible Vepolink products and services.

It outlines the approach to service availability, technical support, issue prioritization, response targets, maintenance, and customer responsibilities.

The actual service commitments for a particular customer may be defined in the applicable quotation, purchase order, project agreement, warranty terms, or separate support contract.

2. Scope of Services

Depending on the products purchased and the agreed project scope, services may include:

  • Installation and commissioning support.
  • Industrial monitoring system configuration.
  • Data acquisition and communication support.
  • Dashboard and reporting support.
  • Cloud connectivity assistance, where included.
  • Troubleshooting of supported equipment and software.
  • Preventive maintenance and calibration coordination, where contracted.
  • Technical consultation and product-related assistance.

Services not included in the applicable commercial agreement may be subject to separate charges or arrangements.

3. Support Availability

Vepolink aims to provide timely and appropriate technical assistance through its designated support channels.

Proposed standard support hours:

  • Monday to Friday: 9:30 AM to 6:00 PM IST
  • Excluding public holidays, unless otherwise agreed

Actual support hours, emergency coverage, and escalation arrangements will depend on the applicable service contract.

4. Issue Classification and Response Targets

Support requests may be classified according to their impact on business operations and system functionality. The following targets are illustrative and require confirmation before publication:

Priority Description Proposed Initial Response Target
Critical A major service failure affecting essential monitoring operations, with no reasonable workaround available. Within 4 business hours
High Significant functionality is unavailable or degraded, but some operations remain possible. Within 1 business day
Medium A non-critical feature is affected, or a workaround is available. Within 2 business days
Low General questions, documentation requests, or minor issues with limited operational impact. Within 3 business days

Response time means the time taken to acknowledge a support request and begin an initial assessment. It does not guarantee resolution within the same period.

Business-hour calculations, priority assignment, and escalation arrangements will follow the applicable support agreement.

5. Service Availability

Where Vepolink provides a managed cloud service or hosted platform, the applicable service agreement may specify a monthly availability target.

Any availability commitment must identify the service covered, measurement method, measurement period, exclusions, and any applicable service credits.

For illustration, a service-specific agreement might set a monthly availability target of 99.5%. This figure is not a general guarantee of availability for all Vepolink products, monitoring equipment, customer networks, or third-party services.

Availability may be affected by:

  • Scheduled maintenance.
  • Customer network or internet connectivity.
  • Power failures at customer facilities.
  • Third-party cloud or communication service outages.
  • Field instrument or hardware failures.
  • Force majeure events.
  • Other exclusions expressly defined in the applicable agreement.

6. Incident Reporting

Customers should report service interruptions or technical issues through the designated support channels. Where possible, include:

  • Customer or organization name.
  • Affected site, system, or product.
  • Date and time the issue was first observed.
  • Description of the issue and its operational impact.
  • Relevant error messages or screenshots.
  • Recent configuration changes, if any.
  • Contact details for the authorized site representative.

Providing complete information can help reduce investigation time.

7. Incident Resolution and Escalation

After receiving a support request, Vepolink will assess the reported issue, determine its priority, and take appropriate steps within the agreed service scope.

Resolution may require remote troubleshooting, additional information, on-site inspection, replacement parts, third-party coordination, or customer approval.

If an issue cannot be resolved through the initial support channel, it may be escalated to the relevant technical or management team in accordance with the applicable support arrangements.

Resolution times depend on the nature of the issue, equipment availability, access to the affected site, and other relevant factors.

8. Planned Maintenance

Maintenance may be required to preserve system reliability, apply updates, improve performance, or address security concerns.

Where practicable, customers will be notified of planned maintenance that may materially affect an applicable managed service.

Emergency maintenance may be performed when necessary to address urgent security, stability, or operational risks. Maintenance notification periods and permitted maintenance windows may be specified in the relevant service agreement.

9. Customer Responsibilities

Customers are expected to:

  • Provide accurate information when submitting support requests.
  • Maintain suitable power, network connectivity, and site conditions.
  • Provide authorized personnel and reasonable access when required.
  • Follow applicable operating and maintenance instructions.
  • Maintain valid licenses, subscriptions, and support entitlements.
  • Notify Vepolink of relevant system changes that may affect support.
  • Maintain appropriate backups and access controls for systems under their control.

Delays caused by unavailable customer personnel, restricted site access, incomplete information, or conditions outside Vepolink's control may affect service delivery.

10. Exclusions

Unless specifically included in a written agreement, support commitments may not cover:

  • Failures caused by misuse, unauthorized modifications, or improper installation.
  • Problems arising from unsupported hardware or software.
  • Customer network, power, or infrastructure failures.
  • Third-party service interruptions outside Vepolink's control.
  • Physical damage, environmental conditions, or events beyond specified operating limits.
  • Consumables, spare parts, calibration, or on-site visits not covered by the applicable agreement.

Warranty coverage and exclusions are governed by the relevant product warranty and commercial documentation.

11. Service Reporting and Review

For contracted services, Vepolink and the customer may review support requests, recurring incidents, service performance, and opportunities for improvement.

Periodic reports, service reviews, and operational metrics will be provided where specified in the applicable agreement.

12. Changes to the SLA

Service commitments may be revised through mutual written agreement or in accordance with the applicable contract.

Any general updates to this webpage will not automatically override a separately executed agreement unless permitted by that agreement.
