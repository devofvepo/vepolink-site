## How It Works

**Eyebrow:** How it works

**Heading:**

From probe to plant head,  
under five seconds.

**Body:**

One architecture, every site. Wired in over a weekend; tuned to your consent conditions in the first week.

**Steps:**

1. **Field analyser**  
   pH, turbidity, DO, ORP, conductivity, temperature, ammonia, BOD/COD probes.

2. **Plug-and-play DAS**  
   Multi-protocol data acquisition with onboard buffering and field configuration.

3. **Vepolink cloud**  
   Serverless ingest, station tagging, time-series storage, REST and OPC-UA outputs.

4. **Live dashboard**  
   Operators, supervisors, plant heads and regulators each get the view they need.

5. **Alerts & reports**  
   Pre-threshold messages, automated forms, and third-party API pushes.
