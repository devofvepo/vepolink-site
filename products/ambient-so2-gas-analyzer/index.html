01 · Overview

Sulphur dioxide, to the last ppb.

A continuous ambient sulphur dioxide analyzer built on the UV fluorescence method - the accepted technique for regulatory SO2 measurement. A long-life UV lamp with low sensitivity drift, a built-in kicker to remove hydrocarbon interference and specially coated sample paths give a stable, low-noise reading at ambient concentrations.

The instrument reports instant SO2, one-minute, hourly and 24-hour averages alongside sample flow, chamber temperature and pressure, lamp intensity, zero/span status and fault diagnostics.

02 · Measurement principle

How UV fluorescence counts SO2.

Sulphur dioxide absorbs ultraviolet light and re-emits it at a longer wavelength. Fluorescence intensity is proportional to concentration - a direct optical measurement with no reagents or consumables.

UV fluorescence

Excite. Emit. Count.

A UV lamp excites SO2 molecules. A photomultiplier counts the emission and converts intensity to concentration.

Interference removal

Hydrocarbon kicker

A built-in kicker strips aromatic hydrocarbons upstream of the reaction chamber, leaving the SO2 signal clean.

Stability

Auto zero & compensation

Automatic zero correction re-establishes the baseline, with temperature and pressure compensation.

Data path

Logging & reporting

One-minute averages are stored for up to a year and transmitted over Ethernet, RS-232, RS-485, USB and analog output.

05 · FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What is the working principle?

UV fluorescence. SO2 excited by ultraviolet light emits fluorescence in proportion to concentration.

How is hydrocarbon interference handled?

A built-in kicker removes aromatic hydrocarbons before the reaction chamber.

What ranges are available?

0-0.1, 0-0.5 and 0-1 ppm, selectable from the touchscreen.

Is it suitable for continuous monitoring?

Yes. Automatic zero correction supports real-time, uninterrupted operation.

How much data does it hold?

The internal logger stores up to one year of one-minute average data.

How does it integrate with a station?

Ethernet, RS-232, RS-485 and analog outputs connect to data loggers, SCADA and regulatory servers.
