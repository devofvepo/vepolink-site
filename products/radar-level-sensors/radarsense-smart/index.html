The measurement.
The method.

80 GHz FMCW radar

A frequency-modulated continuous-wave radar signal in the 77–81 GHz band reflects from the surface. The frequency difference is used to calculate distance; a configured datum converts distance to water level.

Tilt and internal-humidity readings help identify mounting movement or condensation remotely.

The narrow 8° beam helps at confined mounting locations; verify a clear path to the surface.

Onboard statistics support trend analysis; discharge calculation depends on a site-specific rating relationship.

Typical applications

  • Flood-warning networks
  • Tidal and coastal level
  • Stormwater basins
  • Reservoirs and dams
  • Stream gauging with a configured rating curve

Plan the installation.
Keep the reading dependable.

Water level depends on the mounting datum and a clear path to the actual surface.

Mounting and integration

Mount above the highest expected level, align the sensor and keep the 8° beam clear. Configure the level datum, sampling interval and receiving protocol. Record the initial tilt and humidity readings so later changes can be interpreted. The stated absence of a measurement blanking distance does not remove the need for physical mounting clearance.

Inspection and verification

Review tilt, humidity and level data remotely, then inspect the mounting, radome, connector and beam path at an interval suited to the site. Check the reading against a reference when commissioning or after movement. The brochure's temporary lower-housing inundation statement is not a general immersion rating.

Confirm the exact output, protocol and supply against the receiving hardware. Connect to Vepolink logging and monitoring through a suitable configuration.
