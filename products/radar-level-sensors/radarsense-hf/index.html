The measurement.
The method.

High-frequency 80 GHz FMCW radar

The 77–81 GHz FMCW measurement determines distance to the surface. Compared with the standard RadarSense sensor, the HF option increases selectable scan rate so rapid changes can be captured before an averaging interval reports the level.

Select 1, 2, 4 or 8 scans per second to match the site’s rate of change and reporting needs.

Adjustable averaging over 1–60 seconds balances short-lived movement against a stable reported value.

Tilt and internal-humidity readings remain available alongside the level measurement.

Typical applications

  • Flash-flood warning
  • Dam and gate operations
  • Tidal bores and storm surge
  • Hydraulic structures
  • Urban stormwater surges

Technical specifications.

The up-to-8-scans/s mode is the distinguishing HF configuration. Confirm active current and data-system capacity at the selected scan rate. The brochure’s temporary lower-housing flood tolerance does not establish permanent immersion suitability.

Plan the installation.
Keep the reading dependable.

Water level depends on the mounting datum and a clear path to the actual surface.

Mounting and integration

Mount above the highest expected level with a clear 8° beam. Set the datum, scan rate and averaging window for the site, then confirm the receiver, logger memory, telemetry bandwidth and power budget can support the chosen sustained rate. Record initial tilt and humidity readings.

Inspection and verification

Inspect the mounting, radome, connector and beam path and verify level against a reference. Review remote tilt and humidity readings for changes. Recheck the scan and averaging settings after changing alarms or telemetry. Temporary flood tolerance of the lower housing is not a permanent immersion rating.

Confirm the exact output, protocol and supply against the receiving hardware. Connect to Vepolink logging and monitoring through a suitable configuration.
