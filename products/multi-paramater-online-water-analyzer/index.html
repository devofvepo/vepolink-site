01 · Overview

One analyzer for your whole water profile.

A high-performance online water analyzer that delivers accurate, reliable measurement of up to seven parameters at once across a broad set of water-quality indicators using state-of-the-art optical technology.

Built for exceptional stability, low operating cost and high precision, it suits demanding environments from wastewater treatment plants to river monitoring stations. The modular platform lets your site configure the parameters it needs today and expand later with compatible probes.

02 · How it works

Four proven measurement principles.

UV spectroscopy enables rapid, reagent-free analysis of key water-quality parameters. Complementary methods cover solids, oxygen and pH from a single platform.

UV-Vis spectroscopy

Absorbance - Beer-Lambert Law

COD · BOD · TOC · TSS · Nitrate · Color

A high-resolution spectrograph measures the difference between incident light and transmitted light through the sample.

Nephelometry

IR light scatter - ISO 7027

Turbidity · TSS

An infrared beam passes through the sample and scattered-light intensity converts to a turbidity signal.

Visible fluorescence

Oxygen quenching

Dissolved oxygen

A blue LED excites a fluorescent layer; oxygen through the membrane quenches fluorescence for direct DO measurement.

Potentiometry

Nernst equation

pH

pH is measured as the potential difference between reference and sensing half-cells across a glass membrane.

08 · FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

How many parameters can it measure at once?

Up to seven parameters simultaneously, with the platform configurable for up to 20 parameters of choice.

Does it need reagents or chemicals?

No. Core measurement is reagent-free and uses optical UV-Vis spectroscopy. DM water is required for the automatic cleaning cycle.

How does it connect to SCADA?

MODBUS RS485 is standard. A 4-20 mA analog output, programmable relay and USB port are available as options.

Is it suitable for harsh installations?

The enclosure is rated IP65 and operates from 0-50 °C, with storage down to -10 °C.

Can it measure Total Nitrogen?

Yes. Total Nitrogen is derived from ammonia, nitrate and nitrite measured by the analyzer.

What sample connections are required?

A straight-through hard-pipe inlet ø8 and outlet ø12. Optical path length is selectable from 1 to 65 mm.
