01 · Overview

Carbon monoxide, resolved optically.

A continuous ambient CO analyzer built on non-dispersive infrared gas filter correlation. A long-path curved-mirror optical cell strengthens the absorption signal while a no-leak spinning reference wheel separates carbon monoxide from source drift.

The analyzer displays instant readings, one-minute, hourly, eight-hour and 24-hour averages alongside sample flow, cell temperature, infrared source intensity, zero and span status, and fault diagnostics. Ethernet, RS-232, RS-485, USB and analog interfaces connect the same measurement to a station logger, SCADA system and regulatory server.

02 · Measurement principle

How NDIR GFC counts CO.

Carbon monoxide absorbs infrared light at a specific wavelength. Comparing absorption through a spinning gas filter correlation wheel against a reference gives a signal proportional to concentration.

NDIR

Long-Path Absorption

IR source · curved mirror cell · detector

Infrared light travels a folded, long optical path through a curved-mirror cell holding the sample. CO absorbs a characteristic wavelength; the detector reads the resulting attenuation.

Absorption ∝ CO concentration

Correlation

Gas Filter Correlation Wheel

Spinning reference / measure filter

A spinning wheel alternates a CO-filled reference cell and an empty measure cell across the beam. The differential between the two signals is a self-referencing measurement, immune to slow drift in the source.

Differential signal ∝ CO

Stability

Auto Zero & Compensation

Automatic baseline · T and P correction

An automatic zero correction cycle re-establishes the baseline without operator intervention, while temperature and pressure compensation holds the reading steady as ambient conditions move through the day.

Unattended long-term operation

Data path

Logging & Reporting

Ethernet · RS-232 / 485 · USB · analog

Readings are stored internally as one-minute averages for up to a year and transmitted live to a station data logger, plant SCADA or a pollution control board server.

Download over USB or network

03 · Sample path

From inlet to reading.

Each stage preserves a clean optical measurement: filtered ambient air, a long absorption path, alternating reference states and a corrected reported value.

  1. 01

    Filtered intake

    Ambient air enters through a particulate filter and inert sample line at controlled flow.

  2. 02

    Long-path cell

    A curved-mirror optical cell creates a long absorption path inside a compact housing.

  3. 03

    GFC wheel

    The spinning gas filter correlation wheel alternates reference and measure signals through the same optics.

  4. 04

    Detect and report

    The differential is corrected for temperature and pressure, then displayed, logged and transmitted.

07 · FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What is the working principle?

Non-dispersive infrared gas filter correlation, using a long-path curved-mirror cell and a spinning reference and measure wheel.

How does the correlation wheel improve accuracy?

Alternating reference and measure filters through the same optics cancels slow source drift. The wheel uses a durable no-leak construction.

Which parameter does it measure?

Ambient carbon monoxide for continuous regulatory, industrial, traffic and research monitoring.

What ranges are available?

0-50 ppm and 0-100 ppm, selectable from the touchscreen for background, urban or near-source conditions.

Is it suitable for continuous monitoring?

Yes. Automatic zero correction supports real-time, uninterrupted operation without an operator continuously present.

How much data does it hold?

The internal NAND-flash logger stores up to one year of one-minute averaged data, exportable over USB or network.

Can it be installed outdoors?

Yes, inside a weather-protected station cabinet. The analyzer itself is a rack instrument.

How does it integrate with a station?

Ethernet, RS-232, RS-485, USB and analog output connect it to station loggers, SCADA systems and regulatory servers.

Does it align with CPCB monitoring requirements?

The analyzer is designed for CPCB-aligned ambient monitoring and reporting workflows. Final system compliance depends on project configuration, siting and calibration practice.

What maintenance does it need?

Inlet filter changes, leak and flow checks, optical inspection, and scheduled zero and span verification with certified CO gas. No reagents are required.
