01 · Overview

In-situ precision, installed on one side.

A tunable semiconductor laser scans the absorption line of the measured gas; concentration is read from the second harmonic signal. The result is a non-contact measurement with stable zero at low concentration.

One flange carries the probe and analyzer head. The beam returns from an in-duct reflector across the measuring gap, so there is no opposed mounting or optical-path adjustment to remake after thermal movement.

Scan the line, read the harmonic.

07 · FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What is the In-Situ TDLAS Gas Analyzer?

It is a high-precision industrial analyzer that uses tunable diode laser absorption spectroscopy for direct, non-contact gas measurement inside a duct or process line.

How does it measure gas concentration?

A tunable laser scans a selected absorption line. The receiver processes the second-harmonic signal and calculates concentration without extracting or conditioning a sample.

What are the main operational benefits?

Sub-second response, low drift, no moving sampling components, online calibration and minimal maintenance make it suitable for both emissions reporting and process control.

Is installation complicated?

No. The folded optical path uses one mounting flange, with the probe and reflector installed from the same side and no opposing heads to align.

Where is it typically used?

Applications include continuous emissions monitoring, combustion and process-gas analysis in power, petrochemical, cement, steel, manufacturing and waste-treatment plants.

How is the analyzer calibrated?

An internal reference gas cell supports online verification and calibration without disassembly or interruption of the process measurement.

What measuring range is available?

The range and measuring gap are configured at order for the target gas, expected concentration and process conditions.

How does it compare with a cross-duct analyzer?

Both use TDLAS in situ. Cross-duct systems suit very large ducts, while this folded one-side arrangement gives a faster, simpler installation for moderate measuring gaps.
