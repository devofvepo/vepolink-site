The probe senses.
Your system puts it to work.

A probe measures in the water. The receiving instrument supplies the interface for display, logging or control. Some digital probes also process measurements internally.

  1. Probe in the water

    Senses the selected parameter

  2. Analyzer, controller or logger

    Receives and manages the signal

  3. Monitoring and control

    Displays, records or acts on the result

Digital connections

RS-485 Modbus is specified for many sensors in the lineup. Check the selected sensor’s protocol, supply voltage and register mapping against the receiver. Confirm any interface that is not specified on its technical page.

Analog connections

Analog pH, ORP, EC and TDS electrodes need a suitable measuring input. A transmitter can provide a 4–20 mA plant signal when specified. Match the electrode, transmitter and receiver rather than assuming every analog probe supplies a current loop.

Before you
specify a probe.

Can a probe work with my existing analyzer or PLC?

Compatibility depends on more than the connector. We check the parameter, output, protocol and register mapping, power supply and wiring against your receiving instrument. Send the analyzer or PLC model with your enquiry.

Can I mix digital and analog probes in one installation?

Yes, when the receiving system provides the appropriate digital interfaces and analog inputs. Each selected probe must be checked against those inputs and its power requirements.

How deep can the probes be installed?

Immersion depth and pressure depend on the model. IP68 alone does not give a universal depth limit, and some assemblies are designed for flow-through indoor use. Tell us the installation depth or sample-flow arrangement when requesting a configuration.

Which cable lengths and mounting options are available?

Cable lengths differ by sensor: the technical pages list the standard cable and any stated extension options. Mounting may use an electrode holder, immersion bracket or flow cell. Confirm the cable run, receiver and mounting together.

How often do probes need cleaning or calibration?

The interval depends on the sensing method and water quality. Plan access for cleaning and verification against suitable standards. An automatic wiper is an option on selected probes, not a feature of every model.

Does a probe only send a raw signal?

The probe senses the water and sends an electrical or digital output. Some digital probes also condition signals and process measurements internally. An analyzer, controller or logger provides the receiving interface and the display, logging or control functions your system needs.

What should I confirm before ordering?

Confirm the range, reporting units, accuracy conditions, output, supply and mounting for the supplied configuration. Where source documentation differs between versions, the current model datasheet should settle the final specification.
