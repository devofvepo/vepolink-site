The measurement.
The method.

Measuring electrode against a reference electrode

The measuring electrode and reference establish an electrical potential in the sample. The result describes oxidation-reduction conditions in millivolts and is distinct from a conductivity measurement.

Continuous redox potential measurement across a ±2000 mV range.

Polypropylene housing with an Ag/AgCl reference gel.

Optional conversion module for an RS-485 connection.

Typical applications

  • Disinfection control
  • Water treatment
  • Industrial process water

Mounting and integration

Use a receiver designed for an ORP electrode. Confirm the terminal connection and any transmitter needed for the plant input.

Cleaning and verification

Keep the electrode and reference junction free of deposits. Verify response with a suitable redox standard; the listed quinhydrone values are reference checks, not measurement accuracy.
