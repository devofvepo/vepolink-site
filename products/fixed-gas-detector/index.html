01 · Overview

Protection that never clocks off.

The Vepolink fixed gas detector watches one hazardous gas continuously at the point a release is most likely. A high-brightness LED presents concentration locally while analogue, digital and relay outputs carry the same event to alarms, PLCs and safety shutdown systems.

Its Ex d IIC T6 flameproof enclosure and IP65 protection suit demanding process areas. More than 20 plug-in sensor heads let one transmitter platform cover combustible gases, oxygen and a broad range of toxic gases.

02 · Detection system

From gas release to decisive action.

The detector combines the appropriate sensor chemistry with local processing, plant-wide communication and a containment-rated enclosure.

Electrochemical

Toxic gases & oxygen

CO · H₂S · SO₂ · NH₃ · NOx · O₂

Target gas reacts at the sensing electrode and produces a current proportional to concentration. Sensor chemistry provides selective ppm-level measurement with low power demand.

Catalytic

Flammable gases

CH₄ · C₃H₈ · C₂H₂ · H₂

Combustible gas oxidises on a heated bead. The resistance change against an inert reference becomes a stable percentage of the lower explosive limit.

Signal path

Loop to control panel

4-20 mA · 0-5 V · RS-485 · relays

One instrument can drive a standard current loop, voltage output, serial network and local relay actions. Optional wireless extends reporting where cable runs are impractical.

Protection

Flameproof by design

Ex d IIC T6 · IP65 · M45×2

The enclosure contains any internal ignition and cools escaping gases through the flame path. IP65 sealing resists dust and water jets in outdoor process areas.

05 · Installation & upkeep

Installed once. Trusted every day.

A fixed detector only protects the plant when location, wiring, alarm logic and calibration are treated as one engineered safety loop.

01 · Location

Follow the gas

Mount low for gases heavier than air and high for lighter gases, close to credible leak sources and away from dead zones.

02 · Orientation

Sensor downward

Keep the sensing head vertical and downward to limit dust, water and condensate reaching the diffusion face.

03 · Gland

Preserve Ex integrity

Use an approved flameproof cable gland, correct thread engagement and an effective protective earth.

04 · Panel test

Prove the whole loop

Confirm local display, current output, serial data, alarms, relays and shutdown action before handover.

05 · Calibration

Gas through the cap

Apply certified zero and span gas through the calibration cap on the service schedule and after sensor replacement.

06 · Sensor head

Plug in and restore

Replace the modular head at end of life, then recalibrate and function-test the complete safety chain.

Fixed and portable together

Fixed detectors continuously protect known release points. Portable detectors travel with personnel and verify confined spaces, maintenance areas and changing work fronts.

Scaling a system

Standardise sensor heads, output protocols and alarm philosophy across the site, then connect every transmitter to a common panel, PLC or environmental monitoring platform.

07 · FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What does a fixed gas detector do?

It continuously measures one selected gas at a permanent location and sends concentration and alarm status to the plant control system.

Which gases can it detect?

More than 20 sensor heads cover flammable gases, oxygen and common toxic gases including CO, H₂S, SO₂, NH₃, NO₂, chlorine and ozone.

Is it suitable for hazardous areas?

Yes. The transmitter is rated Ex d IIC T6 with IP65 ingress protection when installed with approved glands and practices.

How does it connect to a control panel?

Available outputs include 4-20 mA, 0-5 V, RS-485 and alarm relays, with optional digital wireless communication.

Where should it be installed?

Near credible leak sources and at a height based on gas density: generally low for heavier gases and high for lighter gases.

How fast does it respond?

The stated response time is 30 seconds, with a 10-second recovery time.

Does it store readings?

Yes. The detector includes 1 MB of non-volatile logging memory and an RS-232 service interface.

How is the detector calibrated?

Certified zero and span gas is applied through the calibration cap; the complete alarm and output loop should then be verified.
