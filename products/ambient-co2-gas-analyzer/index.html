01 · Overview

Carbon dioxide, across two ranges at once.

A continuous ambient CO2 analyzer built on non-dispersive infrared gas filter correlation, with dual sensor channels covering 0-5% and 0-30% CO2 from one instrument. Curved mirrors create long optical paths in compact cells, while no-leak GFC wheels keep both signals stable over a long service life.

A 32-bit ARM processor runs on Linux with a 7-inch touchscreen, automatic zero correction, and Ethernet, RS-232, RS-485, USB and analog interfaces - reporting to a station, SCADA and a regulatory server at once.

02 · Measurement principle

How NDIR GFC counts CO2.

Carbon dioxide absorbs infrared light at a specific wavelength. Comparing absorption through a spinning gas filter correlation wheel against a reference gives a direct optical signal proportional to concentration.

NDIR

Long-Path Absorption

IR source · curved mirror cell · detector

Infrared light travels a folded, long optical path through a curved-mirror cell holding the sample. CO2 absorbs a characteristic wavelength; the detector reads the resulting attenuation.

Absorption ∝ CO₂ concentration

Dual range

Two NDIR Channels

0-5% channel · 0-30% channel

The shared sample splits across two independent NDIR sensor channels, one tuned for ambient CO2 and one for elevated concentrations, so both ranges report from one instrument.

One sample, two ranges

Stability

Auto Zero & Compensation

Automatic baseline · T and P correction

An automatic zero correction cycle re-establishes the baseline without operator intervention, while temperature and pressure compensation holds the reading steady as ambient conditions move through the day.

Unattended long-term operation

Data path

Logging & Reporting

Ethernet · RS-232 / 485 · USB · analog

Readings are stored internally as one-minute averages for up to a year and transmitted live to a station data logger, plant SCADA or a pollution control board server.

03 · Sample path

From inlet to reading.

Everything between the sample line and display keeps flow constant, the optical path clean and both channels reporting the same instant of air.

  1. 01

    Filtered intake

    Ambient air passes a 47 mm Teflon particulate filter and inert sample line into the analyzer.

  2. 02

    Critical-orifice flow

    A critical orifice stabilises sample flow at 800 cc/min ±10% for a consistent draw.

  3. 03

    Dual NDIR cells

    The sample fills two curved-mirror cells, each paired with a GFC wheel tuned to its own range.

  4. 04

    Detect and report

    Both channels are corrected for temperature and pressure, then displayed, logged and transmitted.

Racked, calibrated, left running.

The analyzer is installed in a station cabinet or process rack with the sample manifold, calibration source and data logger. A critical orifice holds flow at 800 cc/min ±10%, with scheduled zero and span checks across both ranges.

07 · FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What is the working principle?

Non-dispersive infrared gas filter correlation, using long-path curved-mirror cells and spinning correlation wheels for self-referencing CO2 measurements.

Can it measure low CO2 concentrations accurately?

Yes. The 0-5% channel is designed for reliable measurement at ambient levels, with a lower detectable limit below 0.2 ppm.

What are the two measuring ranges for?

The 0-5% channel covers ambient and HVAC monitoring; the 0-30% channel provides headroom for process vents, greenhouses and confined spaces.

Does it support automatic calibration?

Automatic zero correction holds a stable baseline on both channels without operator intervention.

Is it suitable for continuous monitoring?

Yes. Automatic zero correction supports real-time, uninterrupted operation without an operator continuously present.

How much data does it hold?

The internal NAND-flash logger stores up to one year of one-minute averaged data, exportable over USB or network.

Can it be installed outdoors?

Yes, inside a weather-protected station cabinet. The analyzer itself is a rack instrument.

How is sample flow controlled?

A critical orifice stabilises flow at 800 cc/min ±10%, keeping residence time and calibration consistent in each optical cell.

What interfaces are available?

Ethernet, USB 2.0, RS-232, RS-485 and optional analog output support flexible station integration.

What maintenance does it need?

Inlet filter changes, leak and flow checks, optical inspection, and scheduled zero and span verification with certified CO2 gas. No reagents are required.
