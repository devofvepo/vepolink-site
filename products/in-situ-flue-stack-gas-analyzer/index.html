01 · Overview

Measure hot gas where it flows.

This highly integrated analyzer combines NDIR and DOAS optical absorption to resolve multiple gases inside the stack. Nothing is cooled, condensed or transported through a remote sample line.

A single mounting flange carries the SS316L probe and analyzer. Heat tracing preserves the gas condition, while automatic purge clears dust and salts from the gas path.

02 · Measurement principle

Two optical methods, one gas path.

NDIR quantifies infrared-active gases while DOAS separates characteristic absorption structures across a wavelength band.

NDIR

Infrared absorption

Gas-specific absorption follows Beer-Lambert behaviour, supporting CO, CO₂, SO₂ and NO₂ measurement.

DOAS

Differential optical absorption

The differential spectrum resolves overlapping gas signatures for stable multi-gas analysis.

Gas path

Probe, filter and purge

Replaceable filters, a high-temperature flowmeter and automatic purge protect the full 1.8 m probe.

Calibration

Manual or automatic

Scheduled or operator-triggered zero and span checks control drift without opening the analyzer.

In-situ versus extractive

No remote sample conditioning

In-situ measurement keeps the gas hot and avoids a long sample line, cooler and condensate system. Choose extractive when a centralized shelter or complex conditioning is essential.

07 · FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What is an in-situ flue stack gas analyzer?

It measures gas concentration directly inside a flue, stack or pipeline through one mounted probe instead of transporting a sample to a remote cabinet.

Which gases can it measure?

It is configured for CO, CO₂, SO₂, NO₂, NOx and O₂, with standard or application-specific ranges.

How does the analyzer work?

NDIR and DOAS optical absorption quantify characteristic gas signatures in the hot sample path.

Where is it typically used?

Power, cement, manufacturing, boiler, denitrification and other continuous industrial emissions applications.

What are the main features?

Single-flange installation, heat tracing, automatic purge, replaceable filters, digital display, zero/span calibration and RS-485 communication.

How hot can the gas be?

The configured probe and heat-traced gas path support stack temperatures up to 450 °C.

How does it cope with dust?

Automatic purge and replaceable filters clear dust and salt deposits from the measuring path.

What is the probe made from?

The standard 1.8 m probe is manufactured from corrosion-resistant SS316L.

How is data transmitted?

One RS-485 interface connects the analyzer to a logger, PLC or plant monitoring system.

How stable is the measurement?

Repeatability is ±2% F.S., with specified zero and span drift of 2% per month and automatic calibration support.
