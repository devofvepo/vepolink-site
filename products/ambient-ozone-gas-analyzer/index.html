01 · Overview

Ground-level ozone, read continuously.

A continuous ambient ozone analyzer built on UV absorption, the accepted technique for regulatory O3 measurement. A high-efficiency scrubber generates an ozone-free reference stream, while a specially coated specimen tube resists adhesion and preserves trace concentrations from inlet to cell.

The analyzer displays instant readings, one-minute, hourly and eight-hour averages alongside sample flow, cell temperature, UV lamp intensity, scrubber status, zero and span status, and fault diagnostics. Ethernet, RS-232, RS-485, USB, DIO and analog interfaces connect the same measurement to a station logger, SCADA system and regulatory server.

02 · Measurement principle

How UV absorption counts O3.

Ozone absorbs ultraviolet light strongly at 254 nm. Comparing light transmitted through the sample against a scrubbed, ozone-free reference gives a direct optical signal proportional to concentration.

UV absorption

Absorb and Compare

UV lamp · absorption cell · detector

Sample air passes a cell illuminated at 254 nm. Ozone absorbs a fraction of that light proportional to its concentration; a detector measures the transmitted intensity.

Absorption ∝ O₃ concentration

Reference

Scrubbed Baseline

High-efficiency O3 scrubber

A high-efficiency scrubber strips ozone from a parallel air stream to create a true zero reference. The differential between sample and scrubbed reference isolates the ozone signal.

Sample - Scrubbed = O₃

Stability

Auto Zero & Compensation

Automatic baseline · T and P correction

An automatic zero correction cycle re-establishes the baseline without operator intervention, while temperature and pressure compensation holds the reading steady as ambient conditions move through the day.

Unattended long-term operation

Data path

Logging & Reporting

Ethernet · RS-232 / 485 · USB · analog

Readings are stored internally as one-minute averages for up to a year and transmitted live to a station data logger, plant SCADA or a pollution control board server.

03 · Sample path

From inlet to reading.

Each stage protects the ppb-level measurement: filtered ambient air, a low-adhesion sample path, an ozone-free reference and a compensated reported value.

  1. 01

    Filtered intake

    Ambient air enters through a particulate filter and inert sample line at controlled flow.

  2. 02

    Coated specimen tube

    A specially coated tube resists ozone adhesion and preserves the molecule between the inlet and optical cell.

  3. 03

    Scrubbed reference

    A parallel stream passes through the high-efficiency O3 scrubber to establish an ozone-free baseline.

  4. 04

    Detect and report

    UV absorption is corrected for temperature and pressure, then displayed, logged and transmitted.

07 · FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What is the working principle?

UV photometric absorption. Ozone absorbs ultraviolet light at 254 nm in proportion to its concentration, measured against a scrubbed ozone-free reference.

Which parameter does it measure?

Ambient ozone concentration, together with averaging, operating and diagnostic channels.

Is it suitable for continuous monitoring?

Yes. Automatic zero correction and temperature and pressure compensation support unattended real-time operation.

Does it align with CPCB monitoring requirements?

The analyzer is designed for CPCB-aligned ambient monitoring and reporting workflows. Final compliance depends on project configuration, siting and calibration practice.

Can it be installed outdoors?

Yes, inside a weather-protected station cabinet. The analyzer itself is a 19-inch rack instrument.

How does the scrubber support accuracy?

The high-efficiency O3 scrubber creates an ozone-free reference stream, letting the analyzer isolate sample absorption from optical drift.

What ranges are available?

0-0.1 ppm, 0-0.5 ppm and 0-1 ppm, selectable from the touchscreen.

How much data does it hold?

The internal NAND-flash logger stores up to one year of one-minute averaged data, exportable over USB or network.

How does it integrate with a station?

Ethernet, RS-232, RS-485, USB and analog output connect it to station loggers, SCADA systems and regulatory servers.

What maintenance does it need?

Inlet filter changes, leak and flow checks, scrubber-media checks, optical diagnostics and scheduled zero and span verification with a traceable ozone source.
