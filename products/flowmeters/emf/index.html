The measurement.
The method.

Electromagnetic induction

A conductive liquid moving through the meter’s magnetic field induces a voltage at the electrodes. The transmitter converts that signal into velocity and volumetric flow. The full-bore tube has no moving measuring parts; the liquid must fill it completely.

Integral and remote transmitter options let you position the display for access and the process environment.

Forward, reverse and net totalisers support bidirectional measurement. Empty-pipe detection, flow direction and fault diagnostics help with commissioning and operation.

Choose the lining, electrodes and process connection together for the liquid chemistry and operating duty.

Typical applications

  • Water supply
  • Wastewater treatment
  • Sludge and slurry lines
  • Compatible chemical processes
  • Food and beverage processes

Technical specifications.

Liquid conductivity must be above 20 µS/cm. Temperature, lining, pressure and connection options must be specified together; maximum limits are not automatically available in one assembly.

Plan the installation.
Keep the reading dependable.

Liquid conductivity above 20 µS/cm. Select the liner and electrodes for the liquid chemistry.

Mounting and integration

Keep the tube full, provide the specified straight runs and confirm grounding. Check the zero and output scaling at commissioning.

Inspection and verification

Inspect the liner and electrodes for coating, abrasion or chemical attack. Check grounding, zero, flow direction and output scaling during verification. Request the supplied meter’s calibration record and set a periodic verification schedule for the application.

Connect the selected output to compatible receiving hardware, with the correct power, scaling and protocol configuration.
