The liquid decides.
The installation narrows it down.

Start with conductivity and how the liquid moves. Pipe and process conditions determine which meter can produce a reliable reading.

Electromagnetic

A conductive liquid moving through a magnetic field produces a voltage proportional to velocity. EMF and EcoFlow measure this signal through electrodes in a full flow tube.

  • Liquid: conductive water, wastewater or compatible process liquids.
  • Selection: check conductivity, liner or body, electrodes and process ratings.
  • Installation: full pipe, correct grounding and suitable straight runs.
Explore EMF Explore EcoFlow

Ultrasonic

In pipes, SonicLine and ClampWave compare upstream and downstream sound travel times. Above an open channel, EchoFlow times an echo to measure level and then calculate flow.

  • Inline: a dedicated flow tube for suitable conductive or non-conductive liquids.
  • Clamp-on: external sensors; verify the pipe wall and acoustic signal.
  • Open channel: a known flume or weir and the correct head–discharge curve.

Liquid and operating envelope

Confirm the flow range, conductivity, solids, aeration, pressure and temperature. Check every process-contact material, including electrodes and seals.

Mounting and access

Keep pipe meters full and follow the required straight-run guidance. Check clamp-on surface condition or open-channel beam clearance, and plan access for inspection.

Commissioning and upkeep

Verify grounding or signal quality, the zero or level reference, totalisers and output scaling. Set a verification schedule that suits the process and reporting requirements.

Before you
specify a meter.

Electromagnetic or ultrasonic: where should I start?

Start with the liquid and installation. Electromagnetic meters need a conductive liquid and a full measuring tube. Transit-time ultrasonic meters can suit conductive or non-conductive liquids when the acoustic path is suitable. For an open channel, EchoFlow measures level at a flume or weir and derives flow from its geometry.

Can I add measurement without cutting the pipe?

ClampWave uses transducers on the outside of the existing pipe. Confirm the material, dimensions, lining, temperature and signal quality before selection. SonicLine is an inline meter: under-pressure transducer servicing does not mean every initial installation can be completed without a shutdown.

Does EchoFlow’s accuracy describe the final flow reading?

No. EchoFlow measures level and derives flow from a flume or weir’s head–discharge relationship. Flow accuracy also depends on the primary device, its dimensions and condition, the head-measurement location and the configured level-to-flow relationship.

Is EcoFlow suitable for every corrosive liquid?

Compatibility must be checked against the actual liquid, concentration, pressure and temperature. Check all wetted materials against the selected liquid. The plastic body alone does not establish compatibility; the product page identifies the standard body and electrode materials.

Can these meters connect to a PLC, SCADA or Vepolink monitoring?

Select the meter’s output and match it to the receiving hardware. The lineup includes 4–20 mA and, depending on the model, pulse/frequency, Modbus RTU, HART or PROFIBUS PA. Vepolink can configure logging and cloud reporting through a suitable integration; confirm interfaces, power, scaling and register or protocol support together.

What information is needed to size a flowmeter?

Send the liquid, minimum/normal/maximum flow, conductivity, pipe dimensions or channel geometry, pressure, temperature and required outputs. Include solids or aeration, available straight runs, power, access and shutdown constraints. The selected configuration determines the final limits and installation requirements.
