01 · Overview

One analyzer for your whole water profile.

The Vepolink in-situ analyzer connects a configurable family of digital immersion sensors for reagent-free, low-maintenance and continuous online monitoring. Direct-immersion probes remove the need for sample lines or pretreatment.

The VS-UV254 optical core measures COD, BOD, TOC and TSS. Electrochemical, potentiometric, amperometric, fluorescence and optical-scattering sensors extend coverage to nutrients, dissolved gases, oxidants, solids and algae - all over one RS485 MODBUS line.

02 · Measurement

UV-Vis sensing, inside the process.

The probe combines optical absorption and automatic compensation with a choice of two self-cleaning methods: a mechanical wiper or pressurized air.

UV-Visible absorption

Organic matter at the source

COD · BOD · TOC · TSS

Absorbance across the 200-750 nm spectrum is analysed to calculate organic loading and suspended solids.

Dual self-cleaning

Stable readings over time

Mechanical wiper · Pressurized air

Choose an integrated mechanical wiper or a pressurized-air jet to keep the optical window clear and reduce drift and maintenance.

07 · FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Does the analyzer need a sample line?

No. Its direct-immersion sensors measure in place without sampling or pretreatment.

Which parameters can it cover?

The modular range covers up to 18 parameter channels across organics, nutrients, dissolved gases, oxidants, solids, turbidity, algae and core electrochemical measurements.

Are chemical reagents required?

No. Measurement is optical and reagent-free, so there is no reagent consumption or secondary chemical waste.

How is fouling controlled?

The probe can be configured with either an integrated mechanical wiper or pressurized-air cleaning. Colour and turbidity compensation further support stable readings.

Can the configuration be customised?

Yes. Select only the sensors your site needs; VS-UV254 range, optical path and cable length can also be configured.

How does it connect to SCADA?

Digital sensors share one RS485 MODBUS line for real-time integration with the analyzer and plant control systems.
