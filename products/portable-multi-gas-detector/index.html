01 · Overview

The whole atmosphere, at a glance.

A multi-gas detector reads several gases with one instrument. The Vepolink four-gas unit monitors combustible gas, oxygen and two toxic gases continuously and simultaneously - the combination confined-space and mining work demands when the hazard is unknown.

A 32-bit MCU drives a colour LCD showing all four channels at once. Self-adapting sensor management maintains dependable readings while 1,200 on-board records transfer over USB for printing and exposure analysis.

02 · Sensing principles

Four ways to see a gas.

Four channels need more than one kind of sensor. The instrument combines the technologies below so each gas is measured the way its chemistry allows.

Electrochemical

Toxic gases & oxygen

Channels 2, 3 & 4 · O₂ · CO · H₂S

Gas diffusing into the cell is oxidised or reduced at the sensing electrode, producing a current in direct proportion to concentration. Low power, ppm resolution and selective by electrode chemistry.

Catalytic bead

Combustible gases

Channel 1 · CH₄ · LPG · H₂ · hexane

Flammable gas burns on a heated catalytic bead, raising its resistance relative to an inert reference. Extended response covers long-chain hydrocarbons alongside methane.

NDIR

Carbon dioxide & hydrocarbons

Optional toxic-slot alternative

An infrared beam crosses a gas cell at a wavelength the target absorbs. The drop in light reaching the detector gives concentration without oxygen or sensor poisoning.

PID

Volatile organic compounds

Optional toxic-slot alternative

Ultraviolet light ionises organic vapours passing between two electrodes; the resulting ion current is sensitive to the ppb level for solvents, fuels and industrial VOCs.

05 · Use & upkeep

Four sensors, one routine.

Good practice is a four-gas bump test before every shift, scheduled calibration with certified quad gas and individual sensor replacement when any channel slows.

01 · Wearing

In the breathing zone

Clip to a collar, lapel or harness so the sensors sample the air the wearer breathes, with ports unobstructed.

02 · Bump test

Daily, seconds

A short exposure to quad test gas confirms all four channels and the alarms respond before entry.

03 · Calibration

Zero and span

Zero all channels in clean air and span with a certified four-gas mix.

04 · Charging

Overnight, shift-ready

Return to the charger after a shift. A low-battery warning sounds well before shutdown.

05 · Sensor change

User serviceable

Each sensor swaps individually in minutes. Recalibrate the affected channel before service.

06 · Records

Downloaded over USB

Up to 1,200 records export for exposure history, audits and incident investigation.

Multi gas or single gas?

Where the atmosphere is uncertain or an entry permit requires four channels, the multi-gas detector is the right choice. For one known hazard worn all day, the portable single-gas detector is lighter and simpler.

07 · FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What does the multi-gas detector do?

It monitors combustible gases, toxic gases and oxygen simultaneously - four channels on one display.

Which gases can it measure?

Combustibles in % LEL, oxygen, CO and H₂S are standard. The two toxic slots can be configured for other hazards.

Is it suitable for industrial safety monitoring?

Yes. It is designed for confined-space entry, industrial safety and gas-leak detection where explosion protection is required.

Does it support data logging?

Yes. Up to 1,200 records transfer over USB for printing and exposure analysis.

What is the sampling method?

Diffusion suits personal wear; the pumped version draws samples through a hose for pre-entry checks.

How are alarms set?

Each channel has its own retained thresholds. Buzzer, LED and vibration activate together, highlighting the alarming gas.

Can the user calibrate it?

Yes. Zero and span calibration is performed on site with standard four-gas calibration mix.

Is the housing rugged?

Yes. Engineering plastic and an anti-slip rubber jacket provide waterproof, dustproof and explosion-protected construction.
