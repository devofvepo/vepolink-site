## Users

## Product Purpose

Vepolink provides environmental monitoring instruments and connected reporting systems. This website helps technical buyers understand measurement methods, specifications, applications, integration requirements, and the next step for configuring a monitoring station.

## Positioning

Vepolink supplies and configures environmental monitoring probes and analyzers, combining them with its own cloud environment platform, station integration, data logging, regulatory reporting, commissioning, and support. Instruments are sourced according to availability and the client's requirements; Vepolink is the reseller and solution provider, not necessarily the instrument manufacturer.

## Operating Context

Products are evaluated for regulatory ambient monitoring networks, industrial sites, traffic corridors, environmental studies, and research. Buyers need defensible specifications, installation context, communication interfaces, calibration requirements, and downloadable technical material.
