Data Residency Policy

Learn how Vepolink manages data storage locations, processing infrastructure, cross-border transfers, and hosting commitments across industrial monitoring and IoT solutions.

1. Introduction

At Vepolink, we recognize the importance of data residency, data protection, and transparency in how information is stored and processed.

Our solutions may involve industrial monitoring systems, data acquisition systems, cloud platforms, dashboards, and reporting applications. Depending on the service and deployment configuration, these systems may collect, transmit, store, or process operational and business information.

This Data Residency Policy explains our approach to data storage locations, processing, cross-border transfers, and customer responsibilities.

2. What Is Data Residency?

Data residency refers to the geographical location in which data is stored or maintained.

For industrial monitoring and IoT solutions, data residency may apply to information collected from monitoring equipment, data acquisition systems, cloud-based applications, customer dashboards, and associated service infrastructure.

The applicable data residency arrangements depend on the product, hosting provider, system architecture, customer requirements, and contractual commitments.

3. Types of Data

Depending on the services used, the information handled may include:

  • Monitoring Data: Environmental parameters, instrument readings, measurements, and monitoring records.
  • Operational Data: Equipment status, system logs, alarms, timestamps, and diagnostic information.
  • Business Information: Customer details, project configurations, service requests, and technical communications.
  • Account Information: User identifiers, access permissions, and account-related information, where applicable.

The data collected and stored will depend on the specific services and features enabled for the customer.

4. Data Storage Locations

The geographical location of stored data may vary depending on the selected infrastructure and service configuration.

Where applicable, data may be stored on customer-managed infrastructure, locally deployed systems, or third-party cloud infrastructure. Vepolink will communicate specific data residency commitments where they are applicable to a product, service, or contractual arrangement.

Customers with particular geographical, regulatory, or organizational requirements should discuss their requirements with our technical team before implementation.

5. Data Processing and Transmission

Depending on the deployment, information may be processed by monitoring equipment, data acquisition systems, local servers, communication gateways, cloud platforms, or reporting applications.

Data transmission may use supported communication technologies and network connections configured for the relevant solution.

The actual processing and transmission architecture depends on the project design, system configuration, and services included in the applicable agreement.

6. Cross-Border Data Transfers

Some services may rely on third-party infrastructure or service providers operating in different geographical locations.

Where personal data is transferred across national borders, Vepolink will handle such transfers in accordance with applicable legal requirements and relevant contractual commitments.

Customers requiring data to remain within a particular country or geographical region should communicate this requirement before deployment so that the available hosting and architecture options can be assessed.

7. Data Protection and Access Controls

Data residency and data security are related but distinct considerations. The geographical location of data does not, by itself, guarantee its security.

Vepolink aims to handle information responsibly and apply appropriate safeguards based on the nature of the data and the services provided.

Depending on the deployment, relevant safeguards may include access restrictions, account permissions, secure communication, and appropriate infrastructure controls. Specific security measures depend on the implemented system and the applicable contractual arrangements.

8. Customer Responsibilities

Customers are encouraged to identify their data residency requirements before implementing a monitoring or cloud-based solution. Customers should:

  • Communicate any geographical data storage restrictions.
  • Identify applicable regulatory or contractual requirements.
  • Review the proposed hosting and processing architecture.
  • Ensure that authorized personnel have appropriate access.
  • Confirm data retention, backup, and deletion requirements.
  • Notify Vepolink of changes to applicable organizational requirements.

Where customers manage their own infrastructure or third-party cloud accounts, they remain responsible for the relevant configurations under their control, subject to the applicable agreement.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Data retention periods depend on the service configuration, customer requirements, operational needs, and applicable legal obligations.

Where supported, data retention and deletion arrangements may be established through the applicable service agreement or project documentation.

Customers should confirm their retention requirements before implementation, particularly where monitoring records must be preserved for regulatory reporting, audits, or other compliance purposes.

10. Third-Party Service Providers

Certain services may rely on third-party hosting providers, cloud platforms, communication services, or software components.

The geographical location and processing arrangements of such services may be governed by the relevant provider's infrastructure and contractual terms.

Where applicable, Vepolink will consider these dependencies when discussing data residency requirements with customers.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our services, infrastructure, operational practices, or applicable legal requirements. Revisions will be published on this page with an updated effective or last-updated date.

Any specific data residency commitments agreed under a separate written contract will be governed by that agreement.

12. Contact Us

For questions about data residency, hosting locations, data processing arrangements, or deployment-specific requirements, please contact our technical support team.
