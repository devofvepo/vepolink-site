How it works

From room to facilities team,
under five seconds.

Every node feeds one resilient acquisition, scoring, dashboard and BMS-control path.

  1. 01Measure

    A calibrated multi-sensor node samples every occupied zone.

  2. 02Acquire

    PoE and wireless nodes buffer, timestamp and tag every channel.

  3. 03Score

    The worst-performing channel determines each room's IAQ index.

  4. 04Watch

    Facilities teams see zone, floor, building and portfolio views.

  5. 05Act

    Alerts and BACnet/Modbus commands adjust ventilation through the BMS.
