1. Introduction

At Vepolink, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal and business information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, process, and protect information when you visit our website, submit an inquiry, request a product demonstration, or interact with our services.

By using our website, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

2.1 Personal Information

When you contact us or submit a form, we may collect:

  • Name
  • Business email address
  • Contact number
  • Company or organization name
  • Job title or designation
  • Location and business address, where provided
  • Information included in your inquiry or message

2.2 Technical Information

When you visit our website, certain technical information may be collected automatically, including:

  • IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Device and operating system information
  • Pages visited and time spent on the website
  • Referring website or source
  • Website usage and diagnostic information

2.3 Business and Service Information

If you engage with our products or services, we may collect information relevant to your requirements, such as project specifications, monitoring requirements, equipment configuration details, and service-related communications.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected for legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Responding to inquiries and requests for quotations.
  • Providing product information, demonstrations, and technical assistance.
  • Understanding customer requirements and recommending appropriate solutions.
  • Processing business communications and service requests.
  • Improving website performance, usability, and content.
  • Maintaining website security and preventing unauthorized activity.
  • Meeting applicable legal and regulatory obligations.
  • Sending relevant business updates where permitted by applicable law.

We do not use personal information for purposes that are incompatible with the purposes described in this policy unless permitted or required by applicable law.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to support website functionality, understand visitor interactions, and improve the user experience.

You may be able to manage cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website features.

Where required by applicable law, we will obtain consent before using non-essential cookies or similar technologies.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

We may share information with:

  • Authorized service providers supporting our website, hosting, communications, or business operations.
  • Technical partners involved in delivering requested products or services.
  • Professional advisers, auditors, or legal representatives where necessary.
  • Government authorities or regulatory bodies when required by applicable law.
  • Relevant parties in connection with a business restructuring, merger, or acquisition, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Where appropriate, we take reasonable steps to ensure that third parties handle information responsibly and securely.

6. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, loss, or misuse.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We therefore cannot guarantee absolute security of information transmitted through the internet.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, meet contractual obligations, resolve disputes, maintain business records, or comply with applicable laws.

When information is no longer required, we take reasonable steps to delete it or securely dispose of it, subject to applicable retention requirements.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to:

  • Request access to your personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of information where legally permissible.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Raise concerns regarding the handling of your personal information.
  • Submit a privacy-related grievance through the appropriate contact channel.

We may need to verify your identity before processing certain requests.

Our website may contain links to third-party websites, platforms, or services. These websites operate under their own privacy policies and practices.

Vepolink is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites. We encourage you to review their policies before sharing information.

10. Children's Privacy

Our website and business services are intended primarily for business and professional audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in circumstances where such collection is prohibited by applicable law.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our business practices, technologies, or legal obligations.

Any revised policy will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions, requests, or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or the handling of your information, please contact us through the official contact details provided on the Vepolink website.
